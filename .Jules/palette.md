## 2025-02-28 - Accessible form labels and keyboard focus
**Learning:** Adding `aria-label` to form inputs without explicit linked labels significantly enhances the screen reader experience. Furthermore, default focus rings on elements can often be too subtle or styled out, so explicitly adding an `outline` paired with `focus-visible` styling dramatically improves accessibility for keyboard navigation users.
**Action:** Consistently ensure inputs have either `id` paired with `<label>` or explicit `aria-label`s, and implement highly visible `:focus-visible` states across projects to support non-mouse accessibility.
